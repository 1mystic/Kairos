# AI/ML System Design case studies: structured outline (extracted, plain text)

Common structure of every case: 1 problem statement, 2 requirements and constraints (graded rules), 3 component selection by tier, 4 ideal architecture, 5 component lifecycle (when to use or remove), 6 minimal compliant architecture under the budget, 7 problem-solving tips, 8 advanced engineering tips, 9 math and cost matrix.

## Single-case PDFs
1. Spam email classifier: 100M+ emails/day, under 15 ms, 3,000 USD/month; fallback engine (uncertain band 0.40-0.60), stream of user feedback, drift monitor; QPS 1,157 avg and 2,893 peak (2.5x); 28.93 MB/s at 10 KB per email; 29 cores at 100 req/s/core; 30% cache hit -> about 2,025 QPS; PSI example 0.4282 with thresholds 0.10/0.25; precision-first threshold; skew prevention by logging serving features; Unicode normalisation/OCR against evasion.
2. YouTube-style recommendations: 800M videos, 20 videos in under 50 ms, 6,000 USD; funnel 800M -> 500 -> 20; two-tower, ANN (HNSW, IVF-PQ); batch and real-time model servers, online feature store, cache with 5-15 min TTL, resampler; brute force 80,000 s vs two-stage 30 ms; Bayes recalibration p = b p' / (1 - p' + b p'); focal loss; cost 4,722 USD.
3. Stripe-style real-time fraud: 500K tx/day, p99 under 50 ms, 4,000 USD; stream, online feature store, RT model, fallback rules, drift monitor; QPS 5.79 avg and 58 peak; thresholds block at 0.85, step-up between 0.30 and 0.85; delayed chargeback labels (30-90 days) and surrogates; cost 1,150 USD.
4. Amazon-style product recommendations: 500M SKUs, 300M users, 6,500 USD; utility = P(click) P(purchase|click) price (1 - return rate); 128-d vectors to 16 bytes with PQ; QPS 2,314.8 avg and 8,102 peak (3.5x); cost 5,800 USD.
5. OpenAI-style distributed training: 1T parameters, 10,000+ GPUs, MFU at least 60%, 12,000 USD control plane; 3D parallelism, ZeRO, overlap, sequence packing, FP8, async checkpointing, topology-aware ranks, loss-spike rollback; 6N FLOPs per token; 2.4e19 FLOPs per step; 2.021 s per step; 16 TB model state; 1.6 GB per GPU with ZeRO-3.
6. Anthropic-style enterprise RAG: 100M+ chunks, 50M queries/day, retrieval p99 under 60 ms, 10,000 USD; hybrid dense + BM25 with RRF (k = 60), cross-encoder rerank, ACL pre-filtering, CDC cache invalidation, contextual embeddings, score normalisation; QPS 578.7 avg and 2,025.5 peak (3.5x); int8 quantisation 75% memory reduction.
